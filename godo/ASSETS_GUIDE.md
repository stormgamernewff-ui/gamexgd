# Open-Source 3D Models & Realistic Skyboxes (CC0)

This project works out of the box with procedural 3D trees, terrain, fog, and a PhysicalSkyMaterial skybox.
If you want to swap in photorealistic HDRI skyboxes and detailed 3D `.glb` models, download any of these free **CC0** assets and drop them into `assets/skyboxes/` or `assets/models/`:

## Realistic Forest Skyboxes (HDRI / EXR)
- **Mossy Forest HDRI** (Poly Haven, CC0): https://polyhaven.com/a/mossy_forest
- **Rainforest Trail HDRI** (Poly Haven, CC0): https://polyhaven.com/a/rainforest_trail
- **Monk's Forest HDRI** (Poly Haven, CC0): https://polyhaven.com/a/monks_forest

*How to apply in Godot 4.3:*
1. Save the `.exr` or `.hdr` file into `assets/skyboxes/`.
2. Select `WorldEnvironment` -> `Environment` -> `Sky` -> change `SkyMaterial` to `PanoramaSkyMaterial` and drag the `.exr`/`.hdr` into `Panorama`.

## Detailed 3D Forest Models (.glb / .gltf)
- **Poly Haven Trees** (CC0): https://polyhaven.com/models/nature/trees
- **Poly Haven Rocks & Stone** (CC0): https://polyhaven.com/models/nature/rocks-stone
- **Quaternius Stylized Nature MegaKit** (110+ `.glTF` models, CC0): https://quaternius.com/packs/stylizednaturemegakit.html
- **Kenney Nature Kit GLB Pack** (329 `.glb` models, CC0): https://eclair-assets.itch.io/nature-kit-glb-pack-329-free-cc0-3d-models
